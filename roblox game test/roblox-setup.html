Bring Robux extras to Roblox.

A native Roblox shop with optional permanent cosmetics: VIP Factory Owner, Golden Comet Trail, and Nebula Aura.

Download Roblox Studio files ↓
The current game runs on the web. Its HTML files cannot be uploaded directly as a playable Roblox game. The factory world and gameplay must be built in Roblox Studio; this package adds the cosmetic purchase system to that version.

Connect your passes

  1. Publish your Roblox experience under the account or group that should receive its earnings.
  2. In Creator Hub, select your experience and open Monetization → Passes. Create the three passes, set their sale prices, and copy their pass IDs.
  3. Create a ModuleScript named FactoryPasses in ReplicatedStorage. Paste the supplied configuration and replace the three zero IDs with your real pass IDs.
  4. Paste the server script into a Script in ServerScriptService.
  5. Paste the client script into a LocalScript in StarterPlayer → StarterPlayerScripts.
  6. Publish and test your Roblox experience. Prices come from Roblox, and cosmetics are granted only after server ownership checks.

Suggested prices: VIP 99 Robux, Golden Comet Trail 49 Robux, Nebula Aura 79 Robux. You choose actual prices in Roblox. The browser preview cannot accept Robux.

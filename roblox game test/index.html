Welcome to The Factory

Move using WASD, the arrow keys, or the on-screen arrows. Drag the scene to rotate the camera. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Movement follows your camera direction. Press E or the Interact button near a machine or material.

  1. Step on the yellow pad to claim your factory.
  2. Buy the conveyor for $500.
  3. Go outside and collect filament spools.
  4. Return to the funnel beside the conveyor. Deposit your materials to process them and earn money.
  5. Visit the blue research bench inside the factory. Studying uses one spool and permanently increases that material’s selling value by 50%.

The blue arrow points to your next objective. This browser game saves progress on this device when storage is available.

Material Research

Study one spool to permanently increase that material’s selling value by 50%. Studying uses the spool. No money is charged.

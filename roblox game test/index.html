How to play

Turn on Sound to hear Factory After Hours and game sound effects.

Press Space or Sprint for a short speed boost. Move using WASD, the arrow keys, or the on-screen arrows. Hold the right mouse button and drag to rotate the camera, or use Lock mouse to look around freely. Move your mouse to look around; Escape unlocks the mouse. Press Escape again while unlocked to pause. On touch devices, drag to rotate the camera. Use the + and − zoom buttons, press + or − on your keyboard, or scroll or pinch to zoom. Movement follows your camera direction. Press E or the Interact button near a machine or material.

  1. Step onto a marked floor pad to activate it. Purchase pads charge the posted game-money price once. To launch after buying the rocket, step off and back on.
  2. Buy the conveyor for $500.
  3. Go outside and collect filament spools.
  4. Return to the funnel beside the conveyor. Deposit your materials to process them and earn money.
  5. Visit the blue research bench inside the factory. Studying uses one spool and permanently increases that material’s selling value by 50%.
  6. Visit the purple upgrade shop to buy a bigger backpack, running boots, or a better conveyor.
  7. Collect the free net in the material yard and equip it to catch aliens. Buy the rocket for $1,000 and ride to the International Alien Lab to study them. Repair three station relays for rewards and visit the Ship Blueprint Roller to collect ship plans. Find three flight recorders near the Earth outposts and bring them to the station data terminal. In the station, visit three telescope pads to finish a sky survey. Use the return pad to come home.
  8. Complete delivery jobs at the orange dispatch desk for bonus money. Hunt for 8 glowing crystals on Earth and in the lab. Buy BEE-BOT at the upgrade shop for automatic nearby material collection.
  9. Aliens chase and attack in the yard. Press Q or tap Stun pulse to stop nearby aliens briefly, then use your net or sprint away. The med station heals for free. Buy a $650 shield at the upgrade shop to halve damage.
  10. Switch the guide to Expedition to find five survey outposts. Step on each beacon to survey it. The Copper Ridge mine supplies metal for your conveyor. Pick up a crate at the salvage yard and bring it to the dispatch desk to earn $450. Use Teleport to factory to get home.

Press Escape or tap Pause to resume or restart. The blue arrow points to your next objective. This browser game saves progress on this device when storage is available.

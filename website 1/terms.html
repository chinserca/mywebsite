BEFORE YOU ORDER · UPDATED NOVEMBER 1, 2026

Terms of Service

These terms explain how HawkTech3D orders, payments, and replacements work. Please read them before submitting your inventory. If you’re unsure about anything, ask your fundraiser organizer before buying.

Changed your mind? You still pay 50%, and you don’t get the item.

If you buy an item and later regret buying it, you must pay half (50%) of the item’s original purchase price. You will not receive the item.

For example, if your original confirmed price is $6.00 and you change your mind, you owe $3.00 and do not receive the print. This is a cancellation fee, not a discount that lets you keep the item.

The fee is based on your original confirmed student purchase price, not a different base cost shown in the catalog. Contact your organizer to arrange a cancellation. If you’ve already paid or received the item, contact your organizer to arrange the payment adjustment and return; the website does not process refunds or returns.

Who can order

You must be in Grade 2 or above and have a confirmed Gmail account. Give your name, grade, and class so your order can reach the right person. Items may contain small or moving parts and are not offered to younger students. Your school must show you physical samples before you order.

Submitting your inventory

Add the designs and quantities you want, check your details, then accept these terms and submit. Your email is an order request, not an online payment or a guarantee of availability. Submitting prepares an email containing your items, quantities, Gmail address, name, grade, and class. You must review and click Send in your email app or Gmail to send it to Leo. Nothing is sent automatically, and this website cannot confirm delivery. Your organizer confirms the final student price, available colors, and collection arrangements before purchase. Payment is in person.

Prices and printing

The catalog lists the school’s base purchase costs. Your organizer sets your final student price; Leo recommends no more than twice the listed cost. Prices are intended to stay steady and change only when necessary. Custom designs are priced for the specific request.

Final pre-order lists are due Thursday for weekend printing. Production is currently limited to 20 items a week or fewer, so a request may need to wait for another batch. Prints start in a single color. Leo covers failed-print costs, and the school pays only for finished items.

Printing defects and rough-play damage

If an item has a genuine printing defect, such as layers splitting apart, within the first two days, contact your organizer for a free replacement. Damage from rough play does not qualify for a refund or free replacement. A printing-defect request is separate from a change-of-mind cancellation.

Public comments and star ratings

Everyone can read comments. Sign in to post a design idea, comment on an item, or give it 1–5 stars. Your public post shows your first name, comment, and optional rating. Your Gmail address, full name, grade, and class are not part of the public comment feed. Don’t include private information, harassment, or inappropriate content in comments.

You can update your own post for each discussion. Star averages include only submitted ratings; an unrated comment does not lower or otherwise affect the average. Selecting “No rating” removes your previous rating from the average.

Your account and order information

Keep your password private. Account sign-in is handled through Supabase. Your name, Gmail, grade, and class are used for your account and order coordination. Order emails are addressed to leocoobie@gmail.com and sent using your own email app or Gmail when you click Send. Public comments and ratings are stored privately on Vercel and displayed through the website. Draft inventory stays in this browser under your account until you remove it or choose to clear it after sending your email.

Custom ideas and fundraiser questions

Share design ideas through the Comment button or ask your school office or team organizer about a custom print. Allow about three days for a design and test print. The school decides how to use fundraiser proceeds. Questions about orders, cancellations, or these terms can be sent to leocoobie@gmail.com or raised with your organizer.

More printing and fundraiser details
Who can order?

You can order if you’re in Grade 2 or above. These prints can have small or moving parts, so they aren’t offered to younger students. Your school must show you a sample of each design before you order.

How many items can be made each week?

I can make up to 20 items a week for now, sometimes fewer. If you’re pre-ordering, get your choice to your organizer in time for the final Thursday list. I’ll do the printing over the weekend.

What happens if a print fails or breaks?

If a print fails while I’m making it, I cover the cost—only finished items are charged to the school. If yours has a printing defect, like layers splitting apart, within the first two days, let your organizer know and I’ll replace it for free. Please handle your print with care: damage from rough play doesn’t qualify for a refund or replacement. If you change your mind after buying, you must still pay 50% of the item’s original purchase price, and you will not receive the item. The printing-defect replacement policy still applies. See the Terms of Service before submitting.

What goes into the listed prices?

Each listed price starts with $0.025 per gram of filament + $0.03 per printing hour + $0.15 + $1.50. That helps cover the plastic, electricity, printer maintenance, failed prints, waste, and a small profit. The two-sided word needs extra work, so it adds $0.50. I’ve used the rounded prices from my proposal. These are what the school pays for the prints; ask your organizer for your final purchase price.

Is the listed price what I’ll pay?

Check with your fundraiser organizer before paying. The prices here are the school’s purchase costs, and the school sets what students pay. I recommend a student price no higher than twice the listed cost. I plan to keep my prices steady and change them only when necessary.

What materials and colors are used?

I print everything on a Bambu Lab A1 Mini using PLA filament. My proposal describes PLA as non-toxic, biodegradable, and made from natural crops. For now, each print is one color—check the sample to see what’s available. I might try multicolor printing later.

What does the fundraiser support?

Your purchase helps raise money for your school, which decides how to use the funds. If you’re curious about this fundraiser’s goal, ask your organizer. For organizers: very small trial batches are available before committing, and I’m happy to answer concerns or adjust the plan.

Can I suggest a new print?

Yes! Tell your organizer what you’d like me to make, or share what you think of the designs and fundraiser. I check sales each week and may change the collection based on your feedback and the school’s suggestions. Small student surveys from last year and this year helped me choose the first products and prices.

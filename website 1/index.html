04 / BEFORE YOU ORDER

A few things
to know first.

Have a quick read before picking your print. If anything is unclear, ask your fundraiser organizer. Your school can also raise concerns or ask me to adjust the plan.

Who can order?

You can order if you’re in Grade 2 or above. These prints can have small or moving parts, so they aren’t offered to younger students. Your school must show you a sample of each design before you order.

How many items can be made each week?

I can make up to 20 items a week for now, sometimes fewer. If you’re pre-ordering, get your choice to your organizer in time for the final Thursday list. I’ll do the printing over the weekend.

What happens if a print fails or breaks?

If a print fails while I’m making it, I cover the cost—only finished items are charged to the school. If yours has a printing defect, like layers splitting apart, within the first two days, let your organizer know and I’ll replace it for free. Please handle your print with care: damage from rough play doesn’t qualify for a refund or replacement. Pre-orders are non-refundable, including if you change your mind, but the printing-defect replacement still applies.

What goes into the listed prices?

Each listed price starts with $0.025 per gram of filament + $0.03 per printing hour + $0.15 + $1.50. That helps cover the plastic, electricity, printer maintenance, failed prints, waste, and a small profit. The two-sided word needs extra work, so it adds $0.50. I’ve used the rounded prices from my proposal. These are what the school pays for the prints; ask your organizer for your final purchase price.

Is the listed price what I’ll pay?

Check with your fundraiser organizer before paying. The prices here are the school’s purchase costs, and the school sets what students pay. I recommend a student price no higher than twice the listed cost. I plan to keep my prices steady and change them only when necessary.

What materials and colors are used?

I print everything on a Bambu Lab A1 Mini using PLA filament. My proposal describes PLA as non-toxic, biodegradable, and made from natural crops. For now, each print is one color—check the sample to see what’s available. I might try multicolor printing later.

What does the fundraiser support?

Your purchase helps raise money for your school, which decides how to use the funds. If you’re curious about this fundraiser’s goal, ask your organizer. For organizers: very small trial batches are available before committing, and I’m happy to answer concerns or adjust the plan.

Can I suggest a new print?

Yes! Tell your organizer what you’d like me to make, or share what you think of the designs and fundraiser. I check sales each week and may change the collection based on your feedback and the school’s suggestions. Small student surveys from last year and this year helped me choose the first products and prices.

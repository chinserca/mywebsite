Two ways to make it happen.

The school chooses the approach. Leo handles printing, and the school can put the funds raised toward whatever it needs.

01

Pre-order, then print.

The school collects student orders and payment first. Leo prints the exact items on the final list.

  • Final order list due Thursday
  • Printing takes place over the weekend
  • Custom pre-orders have no change-of-mind refunds

02

Try an initial batch of 15 items: five each of three selected designs. The school purchases the batch upfront.

04 / THE PRACTICAL STUFF

Good to know
before we print.

A small, thoughtful program with clear limits and room to grow. The school can raise concerns or suggest changes to the plan.

Who can order?

Orders are limited to students in Grade 2 and above. Samples of each product should be shown to students before they order.

How many items can be made each week?

The current cap is 20 items per week or fewer. For pre-orders, the final list is needed by Thursday to allow weekend printing.

What happens if a print fails or breaks?

Leo absorbs failed-print costs; the school pays only for finished items. Items with a 3D-printing defect within the first two days are replaced for free. Damage from rough play is not covered, and custom pre-orders cannot be refunded for a change of mind.

How are school prices calculated?

The proposed base calculation is $0.025 per gram of filament + $0.03 per printing hour + $0.15 + $1.50. This covers material, electricity, maintenance, failed-print recovery, waste, and a small profit. Two-sided words include an extra $0.50 for processing. The catalog lists the proposal’s rounded school prices.

How much should the school sell items for?

The school sets the selling price. The proposal recommends keeping it at or below twice the school’s purchase price. Prices are intended to stay stable and change only when necessary.

What materials and colors are used?

Items are made on a Bambu Lab A1 Mini using PLA filament. Initial prints use a single color. Multicolor printing may be considered later.

Can the collection change?

Yes. Sales and feedback will be reviewed weekly. Students and school staff are welcome to suggest new designs, styles, and improvements.
